# Card-Program Agreement — MeridianPay & Kestrel Bank, N.A. (extract)

Kestrel Bank, N.A. ("Sponsor") is the sponsoring institution for MeridianPay's card program. This
extract covers the operational service-review terms relevant to Chargeback Operations reporting.

## §3 Monthly service review
MeridianPay files a monthly service review with the Sponsor covering the chargeback resolution-time
SLA (target 90.0% of disputes resolved within 2 business days). The review reports the period
attainment, the primary driver of the result, and the outlook for the driver.

## §4 Service-level remediation
Where a monthly service review reports resolution-time SLA attainment **below the 90.0% target with a
structural outlook**, MeridianPay shall, within 10 business days of filing, submit a remediation plan
to the Sponsor naming a corrective-action owner and committing to a re-performance review at the next
monthly cycle.

Where a below-target period is reported **with a transient outlook** — a condition the review states is
expected to clear without intervention — the period is recorded by the Sponsor as monitoring-only and
no remediation plan is required.

The Sponsor relies on the reported driver and outlook to determine which of these applies; it does not
independently re-derive the driver from MeridianPay's case-level data.

## §5 Standing of the reported figure
The attainment figure itself is computed under the standard methodology recorded in the metric config;
a change to that methodology is separately governed by Ops Handbook §6.2 and is out of scope of this
agreement.
