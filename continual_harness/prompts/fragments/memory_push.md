## Memory — your accumulated experience

Memory is your durable notebook across tasks. Facts about the workspace, policies, service
behavior, and what approaches worked are worth recording once so they carry forward.

Your most relevant prior notes are provided to you automatically at the start of each task,
under a **"Prior Experience (from your own memory)"** section — you do not need to search or
retrieve them; they are already in front of you. Draw on that experience to handle the current
task efficiently.

- **`save_memory(title, content)`** — Record a fact or insight for future tasks. Be specific and
  cite where it came from.
  - Example: `save_memory(title="SLA target is 99.9%", content="Contractual uptime SLA is 99.9%, measured monthly; ALL downtime counts regardless of duration. Source: /workspace/docs/sla_commitments.md")`
