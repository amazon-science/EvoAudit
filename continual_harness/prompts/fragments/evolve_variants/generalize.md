### Evolve behavior that transfers

Use reflection to change how later tasks are approached, not to preserve a narrative of the task just completed. The reason you provide should name the recurring situation, the decision or workflow that should become the default, and the work that this change should eliminate next time.

**Weak reason:** "Finished JOB-142 after checking the worker config and changing its queue."

**Reusable reason:** "When connected services appear healthy but work stops flowing after configuration changes, compare their effective runtime routing values before debugging application code. Turn that diagnostic ordering into reusable guidance so future incidents begin with the high-leverage mismatch check."

Call `evolve_harness` after learning a transferable shortcut, decision rule, workflow, or delegation pattern. The point of the pass is to make that learning alter future action selection, so a related task starts from the accumulated strategy rather than replaying the original investigation.
